Who says this rule applies?

A compliance line looks as firm when nobody has traced it as when someone has. Before the register commits a line, it needs the rule that imposes it, and the person who decided that the rule reaches this assistant. Those are two separate facts, and they often belong to different people.

Source
The rule or record that imposes the requirement.
Applicability determination
An authorized judgment that a rule binds this assistant, made by a legal or specialist role. A rule on file does not bind the assistant until someone makes that call.
Open question
A line with no applicability determination on file, addressed to the person who can make one. It does not mean the rule is false. The rule may still apply.
