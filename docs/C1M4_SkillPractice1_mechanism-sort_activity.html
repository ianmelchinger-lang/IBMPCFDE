Delete the name: what is left?

A requirement that names a product can pass review the day the product is installed, whether or not the need is met. The removal test separates the need from the name. Delete the underlined name, then ask whether the statement still says what the system must achieve, or whether it becomes false or impossible because something on file fixed that condition.

The test has three possible results.

Unchosen mechanism
A product, component or technique offered as one way to meet a need. Other ways are still open.
Fixed constraint
A condition that a documented authority has made non-negotiable. Delete it and the statement becomes false or impossible.
Preference
A wish about how the result should look or sit, with no documented authority behind it.
Documented authority
A recorded fact or decision, from someone entitled to make it, that fixes a condition.
