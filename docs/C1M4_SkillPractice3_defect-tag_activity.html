Could someone else decide it?

A requirement is finished when a reviewer who did not write it can decide whether it was met. When an element is missing, each reviewer supplies their own, and approval records agreement with the wording instead of a result.

Who acts
The system or role that must behave a certain way or show something.
What must be true
The behavior or constraint, stated as one claim.
The conditions
The circumstances in which it must hold, including the hard cases.
The deciding observation
What the reviewer looks at, and the decision rule that turns it into pass or fail. Examining records shows what was recorded. Interviewing shows what someone says is assigned. Testing shows behavior. A method weaker than the claim cannot decide it.
